Tech Adventure v0.1

Open project.godot in Godot 4.3.

v0.1:
- A colorful tech-shop tycoon for phones (portrait, fullscreen on any screen height).
- Start with 30 coins. Unbox the Case Maker (25 coins) and the Worker (free) to open the shop.
- Tap any station for its popup to upgrade it or unlock new parts. Every order needs every unlocked part.
- Upgrades button (lower-left): More customers, Faster cases, Energy, Faster motherboards, Money, Faster screens.
- Quests button (lower-right): Daily and Milestone quests that pay gems.
- Green + button: gem store (hire a second worker).
- Globe button: Worlds. Zokia factory and Zamzung (unlocked at the "techadvv" gate).
- Offline rewards: your shop keeps earning for up to 2 hours while you are away.
- Settings (gear): sound, vibration, floating +coins, compact numbers, reset progress.
- App icon included (icon.png and the icons folder, set up for the Android export).
- Optional "shop is full" notification: see SETUP_NOTIFICATIONS.txt.
